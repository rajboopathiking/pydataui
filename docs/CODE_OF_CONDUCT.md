# Code of Conduct

PyDataUI is dedicated to providing an inclusive, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## Our Standards

We expect all contributors and maintainers to adhere to the Contributor Covenant:

- **Respect & Empathy**: Listen actively, discuss design decisions with civility, and respect differing levels of experience.
- **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer actionable feedback on issues and pull requests.
- **Zero Harassment**: We have zero tolerance for trolling, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter inappropriate behavior in PyDataUI community spaces, please report it to **maintainers@pydataui.org** or reach out directly to the core maintainers on GitHub. All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.
